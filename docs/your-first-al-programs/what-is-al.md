# What Is AL and Why Does Business Central Use It?

## What Is AL?

**AL (Application Language)** is the programming language used to develop and customize applications for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.

It allows developers to add business functionality, extend existing features, and integrate Business Central with external systems.

For example, using AL, you can:
- Create tables to store business data.
- Build pages for displaying and editing records.
- Implement business rules, validations, and calculations.
- Automate business processes.
- Integrate Business Central with external APIs.

**Think of it this way:** Business Central is the business application, while AL is the language you use to extend its capabilities.

AL is designed for business applications, particularly those involving financial management, inventory, sales, purchasing, and other ERP processes.

## Why Does Business Central Use AL?

Business Central already provides functionality for managing finances, inventory, customers, suppliers, and more. However, different businesses have different requirements.

For example, one company might need a custom approval process before a purchase order can be submitted. Another might need to integrate Business Central with its own e-commerce platform.

AL allows developers to implement these requirements without building an entirely new ERP system.

Key benefits include:

- **Customization:** Add functionality to meet specific business requirements.
- **Extensibility:** Extend standard Business Central functionality without directly modifying Microsoft's base application.
- **Business logic:** Implement rules, validations, calculations, and workflows.
- **Integration:** Connect Business Central to external applications and services.
- **Maintainability:** Package custom functionality separately from the standard application using extensions.
